Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 31, 2026

Two Penguins Studios LLC ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information when you interact with our websites, SMS/MMS messaging services, and AI-powered tools.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Contact Information: Names, phone numbers, email addresses, and other information you voluntarily provide.
  • Messaging Information: SMS/MMS messages, photographs, attachments, and other content submitted through our services.
  • Service Information: Questions, requests, descriptions, and other information necessary to provide our services.
  • Technical Information: IP addresses, browser types, device information, and website usage data, where applicable.
  • Consent Information: Records of SMS opt-in, opt-out requests, and communication preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide requested services.
  • Process messages, images, and other submitted information using AI-powered systems.
  • Send service-related SMS/MMS messages and responses.
  • Maintain, improve, and secure our services.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

3. SMS Messaging and Consent

When you opt in to receive SMS communications from Two Penguins Studios LLC, we may send messages related to your inquiries, requested services, and account or service activity.

Message frequency varies based on your interactions with our services. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP. For assistance, reply HELP or contact support@twopenguinsstudios.com .

SMS consent is not a condition of purchase.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We do not share SMS consent information with third parties or affiliates for their independent marketing or promotional activities.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We may use trusted third-party providers, including Twilio, cloud hosting services, and AI technology providers, to operate our services.

These providers may process information on our behalf for the purposes of delivering, maintaining, and securing our services, subject to applicable contractual and confidentiality obligations.

We do not authorize service providers to use personal information for their independent marketing purposes.

5. AI-Powered Processing

Our services may use artificial intelligence to analyze submitted messages, photographs, and other content to generate responses, recommendations, or service-related information.

Information submitted through our services may be processed by third-party AI technology providers acting on our behalf.

6. Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information.

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, fulfill legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

7. Your Privacy Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

To submit a privacy request, email support@twopenguinsstudios.com .

You may withdraw SMS consent at any time by replying STOP.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
